 A WANDERER’S FIELD GUIDE

Bring it home.

Use WASD / arrow keys to move, or middle-click the ground to walk. Hold E near a tree or rock to gather. Gather as many resources as you want. There is no bag weight or carrying limit.

The marked crossing and eastern boss arena allow PvP combat. Click or hold left mouse / Space for a free basic attack. Choose a sword, spear, or axe with 1 / 2 / 3. The sword is quickest, the spear has reach, and the axe sweeps a wider arc. Green outer ground and the central bank are safe.

Hold a direction and press Shift to dodge: 32 stamina initially (reduced by Agility), a one-second recovery, and a brief invulnerability window. Stamina regenerates. Red ground warnings show where attacks will land; move away or dodge at impact.

Thornrunners rush and slash (+25 combat XP). Cinder hunters fire aimed bolts (+40). Ironhide brutes use heavy ground slams (+70). Each is an AI enemy. Combat XP is awarded immediately for a kill; player kills give no XP.

Find The Hollow Warden in the eastern stone arena. Dodge its slams and five-bolt fans; below half health it attacks faster. Recent living contributors near a kill each receive 300 combat XP. It resets after six seconds without a player in the arena and returns 90 seconds after defeat.

Enter the gold bank circle and hold E to deposit. Each wood earns 10 XP; ore earns 14 XP. Skill levels make gathering faster. Banked progress survives death and is stored by the shared world server.

Wagered duels: banking wood earns 5 coins each; ore earns 8. In the PvP crossing, select a nearby player in the Wagered duels panel and offer 10–500 coins each. They must explicitly accept the exact stake. Accepted stakes are locked in a pot; the winner receives both stakes. Only earned game coins are used, with no purchase or cash-out.

Duels move both players to the gold ring with full health and a 3-second countdown. Only the opponent can damage you, and health does not regenerate. Defeat, confirmed forfeit, disconnect, or 2 seconds outside the ring loses the stake. A 120-second timeout or server restart refunds both. Duel defeats keep your bag; disconnecting during a duel stores the bag for the same character to resume with its access key. The initial world has one duel ring.

Skills & workshop (K): train 25 skills across Gathering, Crafting and Combat. Collect wood, ore, fish, berries, herbs, hides, grain, flax, clay and crystals, then bank the haul for XP. Farming uses regrowing harvest patches and Hunting uses snares. The bank workshop has 27 recipes: turn raw materials into supplies, armor, weapons, arrows and spell runes. Higher profession levels unlock advanced recipes; recipe cards show exact requirements.

Keys 4 / 5 select an owned shortbow / ember staff. Their basic shots are free traveling projectiles. Right-click Twin shot costs two arrows, and Frost nova costs one focus rune. Ember runes remain crafting materials for staves and Emberguard gear. Weapon skills train on actual PvE damage and add a modest damage bonus. Defence trains when enemies hurt you; Agility trains on a successful dodge. F spends a focus rune to heal unhealed PvE damage and train Restoration. T eats cooked fish; it shares the supply cooldown with Q / R. Armor works only when equipped in its matching slot. I opens equipment, elemental sets and rarity details. Skills and armor also affect duels, but supplies and Restoration cannot be used there.

Market (M): visit the bank to buy or sell upgraded gear, tonics, rations, and banked resources, or post your own player listing. Goods are held until sold or cancelled; sellers are paid even while offline. Press Q for a healing tonic (+35 health) or R for a trail ration (+45 stamina), sharing an eight-second cooldown. Supplies cannot be used in duels. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 choose the best owned weapon of each type.

High scores (L): lifetime boards rank saved characters in this world, with Overall, PvE, PvP, Boss defeats, Duel knockouts, Woodcutting, and Mining categories. Search by name or rank, browse pages, or choose View your rank. PvE uses combat XP; PvP grants 100 points for a duel knockout or 25 for an open-world player kill. Forfeits and disconnects earn no points. The same opponent grants points once per ten minutes.

If you die, you return to the west grove after 3 seconds. Your bag drops where you fell; hold E nearby to recover it. Disconnecting drops your carried haul too.

Your private access key resumes the same server-stored character on another device. Copy it from Equipment and keep it private. A character can have one active session. Different browsers can create separate characters; this is not verified account registration.

Mouse combat: aim with the mouse. Click or hold left click for a free basic attack, repeating every 0.35–0.5 seconds depending on the weapon. Right-click recovery does not lock the basic attack for several seconds. Right click uses Crescent strike, Piercing thrust, Groundbreaker, Twin shot or Frost nova depending on the equipped weapon. Space remains an attack alternative; B uses the secondary ability. Middle click moves to a point. I opens equipment. Elemental passives burn, shock or chill monsters; base armor/power also affect PvP, but elemental effects currently do not.

The game keeps running while this guide is open. Movement controls pause here; enemies and other players keep moving.

 SCAPE / ARMORY

Forge your identity.

Equipment collection

T1 Common · T2 Uncommon · T3 Rare · T4 Epic · T5 Legendary. Green SET ITEM labels mark synergies independently of rarity. Gear stays after death; your unbanked resources remain at risk.

One piece unlocks its elemental passive. Two and three matching pieces improve it. Legendary items offer stronger individual passives without counting toward sets.

Elemental damage and control currently affect monsters only. Boss stuns last 0.15s; ordinary stuns 0.45s. Base equipment armor and attack power also work in PvP. No proc-on-proc loops.

Pyromancy strengthens burning; Runecrafting strengthens lightning; Enchanting strengthens chill. Defence adds damage reduction. Agility makes dodging cheaper. Restoration uses focus runes for deliberate healing.
